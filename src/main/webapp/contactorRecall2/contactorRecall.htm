ATV Contactor Recall
Warn Industries is voluntarily recalling 8 post contactors that were part of winch kits for All Terrain Vehicles and Utility Vehicles (ATV's). Recall involves 2500 - 3500 lb. winch kits. To request a replacement contactor online please enter the following information.
